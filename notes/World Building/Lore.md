[[Azathoth]] - The Blind Idiot God

The god of creation has created the [[Primordial|Primordials]], and eventually the solar system (with the sun, earth and moon) has formed. 
To protect herself from the forces of chaos (lovecraftian cosmic gods), [[Gaia]] has raised powerful protectors, The ruling [[Overlord|Overlords]] ([[Zeus]], [[Odin]], [[Ra]] and others) 

To maintain her powers, Gaia demands that all earthly creatures have to die one day and return to the underworld, therefore there are gods of death ([[Mot]], [[Thanatos]])

To grow in power and be able to channel the cosmic forces (like the sun) through temples, the overlords have to enforce order on the land, and slay [[Titan|Titans]] and beasts that disrupt their duty to protect earth.

Some outer creatures like [[Cthulhu]] has arrived to the earth and started spreading chaos and weakening Gaia. 
The overlords have managed to trap Cthulhu in [[Dark Sea|The Dark Sea]] and cast eternal sleep on him. But his influence is still prevalent. 

With the help of [[Prometheus]], the gods have made the humans - servants that should help the gods.
