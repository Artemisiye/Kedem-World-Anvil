**Biome**: 
**Tundra**, a **cold, barren landscape** of **permafrost, frozen soil, and sparse vegetation**. 
The tundra is a vast, icy wasteland home to **ancient [[Frost Giant|Frost Giants]]**, relics, and long-forgotten **frozen treasures**. 
Players will experience long **nights**, harsh **blizzards**, and a general lack of life. Few creatures can survive here, but those that do are **massive and powerful**.

**Gameplay Focus**: 
The **Barren Expanse** serves as the primary **home of Frost Giants**, who are sources of **[[Gigantism|Gigantism Stacks]]**. The Giants roam this tundra, and players must tread carefully, choosing when and how to engage. Defeating these massive creatures allows players to acquire **[[Gigantism|Gigantism Stacks]]**, which can improve **strength**, **durability** and **size**. Additionally, players can unearth **frozen relics**, powerful ancient artifacts buried deep within the ice.

**Unique Challenge**: 
Fighting in the tundra is dangerous due to the **freezing environment** and **giant adversaries**. Players must prepare for **high-stakes combat** against **enormous foes**, but the rewards for conquering this land are immense.
