**Biome**: 
**Magical Deciduous Forest** with elements of **mysticism** and **arcane energy**. This forest is full of **ancient, gnarled trees**. 
The forest has a vibrant, enchanted atmosphere, with glowing flora, magical beasts, and strange natural phenomena.

**Gameplay Focus**: 
This biome is ideal for **mages**, **spellcasters**, and players seeking to harvest **arcana** from magical creatures.
