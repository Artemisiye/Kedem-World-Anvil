**Biome**:
**Coniferous Boreal Forest**. 
The environment is more rugged and **wild**, with vast stretches of **dark green canopies** and **snow-covered ground** in colder areas.


**Gameplay Focus**: 
This is a region suited for **warriors, hunters**, and **rangers**. It is full of **large, fearsome predators** and resilient prey. Resources from the forest would be tough and durable, ideal for crafting powerful armor and weapons. **Exploration and survival** play a key role.
