**Description**:: Goblins are small, cunning humanoids engineered by the [[Overlord|Overlords]] as adaptable scavengers and laborers. Designed with traits inherited from [[Coyotes]], they are highly opportunistic, thriving in harsh environments where other humanoids struggle. Goblins form tight-knit clans, valuing survival above honor, and often resort to ambush tactics, trickery, and theft to secure resources.  

Despite their crude and primitive appearance, Goblins are **remarkably intelligent**, capable of crafting weapons, setting traps, and organizing raids. Their chaotic nature often leads them to bicker amongst themselves, but when united under a strong leader, they become a dangerous force.  

Goblins see other humanoids as either **threats or opportunities**, rarely engaging in diplomacy unless it serves their immediate interests. Their settlements are typically makeshift camps or underground warrens, filled with stolen goods and ramshackle defenses.
