**Description**:: Hobgoblins are the larger, stronger kin of [[Goblin|Goblins]], developed from [[Wolves]] instead of coyotes. Unlike their smaller counterparts, Hobgoblins are disciplined, tactical, and highly militant. They form rigid hierarchical warbands, where strength and loyalty determine one's place in the pack.  

Originally created as enforcers and warriors, Hobgoblins excel in organized combat, favoring formation-based warfare over the chaotic skirmishing of Goblins. They craft superior weapons and armor, often raiding human and orcish settlements to bolster their armories.

Though still opportunistic, Hobgoblins value **honor through dominance**, respecting strength above all. They may forge temporary alliances with stronger factions, but their ultimate goal is to **subjugate rather than serve**. Their settlements are fortified encampments or war camps, structured around a central warlord.
