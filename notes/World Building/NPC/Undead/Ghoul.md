**Description**:: Ghouls are undead creatures created by death gods to consume improperly buried corpses, ensuring the natural recycling of mana back into the land. Unlike mindless undead, Ghouls are bound by divine necrotic magic, which halts their decay and sustains their function indefinitely.  

**Behavior**::
- **Corpse Consumption:** Ghouls actively seek out corpses, including fresh remains and lesser undead like zombies.  
- [NpcAggresion::Selective]: They **do not attack the living unless provoked**, but will defend their feeding grounds.  
- **Life Cycle:** After consuming enough corpses, a Ghoul may return to its birthplace to **bury itself** or **dissolve into liquid mana pools** like the [[Styx River]].
