### **Cosmic Hierarchy of Kedem**

#### **1. Supreme Creator**

- **El**: The **God of Creation**, the supreme and unknowable force behind the existence of the cosmos and the Kedem universe. El is distant and removed from mortal affairs, and is not present in any interactions the players may experience.

#### **2. Cosmic/Primordial Deities**

These are the first beings that has formed in the universe from immense mana concentrations, embodying cosmic forces and governing the essential elements of existence. 
- **[[Shamann]]**: The **Sun Itself**, a radiant entity beaming with light and power. Shamann’s light provides strength to the [[Overlord|Overlords]] and [[Gaia]] herself.
- **[[Yorick]]**: The **Moon Itself**.
- **[[Chronus]]**: **Time Itself**.
- **[[Gaia]]**: The **Earth Itself**, a living, breathing entity that encompasses all land, forests, and seas. Gaia's power is bound to the health of the land, and she is both nurturing and demanding — requiring the life of all things to eventually return to her.

**Lovecraftian Primordials**: The stars and planets were not the only ones that formed into existence. From the depths of darkness, unbound-to-matter mana has formed dark and alien deities from beyond the stars, who defy the laws of order and bring chaos:
- **[[Azathoth]]**: **The Blind Idiot God**, the embodiment of chaos and destruction. A devouring black hole that seeks to bring the cosmos to its end. The mere presence of Azathoth warps space and time, threatening the existence of the Primordials.

#### **3. The Ruling Overlords & Opposing Gods**

These are the guardians and rulers of the Earth, raised by [[Gaia]] to enforce cosmic order and protect her from the encroaching chaos of the Lovecraftian Primordials. They are both powerful beings and stewards of humanity's destiny. Each Overlord has a specific domain, often associated with power, control and knowledge.

- **[[Zeus]]** - The **General of the Gods**, lord of the sky and thunder. Zeus rules from his mountain temple, governing the west of the continent, overseeing [[Cthulhu|Cthulhu's]] prison in [[Dark Sea|The Dark Sea]].
- **[[Odin]]** - The **All-Knowing-Father**, a god of wisdom, knowledge, and magic. He sacrificed much to gain knowledge of the cosmic forces, guiding humanity and the gods alike in matters of fate.
- **[[Ra]]** - The **Sun Overlord**, the god of science, light and order, who spins [[Gaia]] to brings the sun across the sky each day. Ra’s power is connected directly to [[Shamann]], who's power is channeled through the sun temples.

Opposed to the overlords are the Great Old Ones:
 - **[[Cthulhu]]** - **The Earth Dweller** is an enormous alien from beyond the solar system that waged war on [[Gaia]] in her early days, which led to the awakening of the [[Titan|Titans]] and the birth of the [[Overlord|Overlords]]. Once defeated, with the help of [[Hypnos]] - Cthulhu was imprisoned for eternal sleep in [[Dark Sea|The Dark Sea]], from which he keeps corrupting the land.
 - **[[Nyarlathotep]]** - **The Crawling Chaos**, a shape-shifting entity that represents deception, madness, and the decay of reality. A manipulator who arrived to infiltrate [[Gaia]] after [[Cthulhu]] was defeated and trapped.

Additionally, throughout the years, various Devils have emerged from various origins, and united under one agenda yet obscure purpose - to lead astray any significant entities from reaching their goals:
- **[[Ben Shahar]]** - **The Lightbringer**, son of the [[Yorick|moon, Yorick]], fallen from the moon to the earth.
- **[[Azazel]]** - **The Deviant**, former Lower God.
- **[[Ashmedai]]** - **The Demon King**, son of Azazel.
- **[[Samael]]** - **The Creeping Death**. Enigmatic god, perhaps the primordial **Death Itself**.
### **Mana & Sentience**

- **High concentrations of mana produce a spirit (soul).**
- **The larger and denser an entity, the more mana it holds, leading to greater intelligence and magical ability.**
- **This applies to celestial bodies (stars, planets), vast natural formations (seas, mountains), and colossal living beings (Archbeasts, Dragons).**

1. **Planetary and Cosmic Sentience**
	- Gaia is alive and conscious because of its immense mana concentration.
	- The Sun, the Moon, and perhaps even **constellations** are sentient beings, capable of influencing the world in mystical ways.
	- Lesser celestial bodies (_comets, asteroids_) might be semi-aware but not fully sentient.

2. **Living Beings & Mana Capacity**
    - **[[Dragon|Dragons]], [[Archbeast|Archbeasts]], and [[Titan|Titans]]** are innately magical, as their massive size gives them deep mana reservoirs and complex souls.
    - **Humanoids have limited mana** and can only use magic through training, artifacts, or external sources (divine blessings, contracts, or consuming mana-rich substances).
    - **Humans** are the prominent race to produce mages among the humanoids.
    - **Normal animals have simple souls** with little to no ability to use mana.

3. **Magic as a Byproduct of Sentience & Soul Complexity**
    - Beings with stronger souls can **manipulate reality directly** by channeling their mana.
    - **Gods, Titans, and Dragons do not need spells**—they simply will changes into existence.
    - **Mortals must refine their crude mana use** through spells, rituals, or runes, since their spirits aren’t naturally attuned to reality-bending.

4. **Natural Formations & Spirits**
    - Vast forests, deep lakes, and towering mountains might **develop guardian spirits** that act as the will of the land.
    - Ancient ruins or battlefield sites with high concentrations of mana could **become haunted** as their residual energy gives birth to sentient wraiths.
    - Spirits/Demons **form naturally** in areas where mana accumulates.
    - **Demons are not evil**, but rather entities that exist only to sustain themselves, following their own instincts.
    - **They have no physical body**—they are living **manifestations of raw mana**.
    - They sustain themselves by **absorbing mana from other beings**, either directly (draining life force and mana) or by consuming lesser spirits.
