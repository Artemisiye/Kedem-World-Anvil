**Description**:: Zeus is one of the [[Overlord|Overlords]], known as the **General of the Gods**, the lord of the sky and thunder.
Zeus rules from his temple in [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]], governing the west of the continent. A significant part of his domain involves overseeing [[Cthulhu|Cthulhu's]] prison in [[Dark Sea|The Dark Sea]]. 

**Domain**:: Sky & Thunder.
