Gigantic beings of immense power, originally created by Gaia as her first protectors, but some have fallen to the chaotic influence of the Outer Gods. Titans often embody the raw forces of nature—mountains, oceans, storms, and more.
Once vanquished, titans do not fully die, and reform over time
