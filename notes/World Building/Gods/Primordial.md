**Description**:: The Primordials are ancient, foundational forces of existence that predate the Overlords and even the structured world itself. They are not [[God|Gods]] in the traditional sense but elemental, cosmic, or conceptual entities—massive concentrations of mana that became sentient over eons. They do not govern domains in service of Gaia but *are* the domains: the sea, the sun, the earth itself.

Primordials are often indifferent to mortal concerns, driven by instincts or incomprehensible wills. Some, like **[[Gaia]]**, embody the world and strive to maintain balance. They can be benevolent, hostile, or entirely alien in motive. Their emergence or awakening often disrupts reality, twisting landscapes and drawing conflict.
