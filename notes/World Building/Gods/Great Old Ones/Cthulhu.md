**Description**:: Cthulhu, known as The Earth Dweller, is an enormous alien entity from beyond the solar system. A Great Old One, Cthulhu embodies cosmic horror and corruption, eternally imprisoned in [[Dark Sea|The Dark Sea]] yet continuously influencing and corrupting the land.
