**Description**::  Gaia is the primordial embodiment of the living world—land, soil, root, and breath. She is not worshiped in temples like the [[Overlord|Overlords]], but her presence is felt in every forest, mountain, and heartbeat. All natural [[mana]] flows from Gaia, and it is through her that the cycle of life, death, and rebirth is maintained.

She is vast, ancient, and mostly dormant, dreaming beneath the crust of the earth. Her will is subtle but ever-present, guiding the instincts of animals, the growth of plants, and the flow of mana through the leylines. Spirits and elementals are often born of her breath, and many creatures, including Archbeasts, revere her as the source of their power.

Though she seldom interferes directly, Gaia creates emissaries in times of crisis—titanic beasts, soul-rich trees, or vast fungal networks—to defend herself from destruction. Her greatest threat comes from chaos beyond the stars and wild [[Primordial|Primordials]] that upset her delicate balance.

Humanoids, crafted by the Overlords, were intended to serve Gaia by shaping, protecting, and restoring her lands. Yet, humans—born of Prometheus—are unique in that they are no longer tied to her cycle, making their role both a blessing and a looming danger.
