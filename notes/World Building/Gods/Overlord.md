**Description**::  The Overlords are powerful divine beings appointed to maintain order in the world of *Kedem* and protect Gaia from the forces of chaos. These include wild elemental titans, aberrant horrors from beyond, and destabilizing rogue spirits. Each Overlord governs a specific domain and operates through influence, servants, and divine constructs—most notably, the **Sun and Moon Temples**, which channel celestial energy to stabilize regions and repel cosmic threats.

While each Overlord has distinct traits and philosophies, they ultimately serve a shared goal: preserving the world's balance. Some work together in loose alliances; others pursue their goals more independently.

They are revered throughout *Kedem*, though rarely seen directly. Their influence is most present in the humanoids they designed, the miracles they enable, and the temples dedicated to their power.
