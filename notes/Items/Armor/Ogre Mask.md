Will [[Cling]] to the player as soon as he wears the mask (the player will not be able to remove the mask at wil, only at death).
Grants 5 [[Gigantism|Gigantism Stacks]], and will harvest additional stacks from dead enemies.
Will consume 1 stack of gigantism per day, and if there are none (excluding the base 5), the mask will consume 20% of the player's [[Vitality]] over the course of the day.

Upon dropping the mask (by dying), the mask will retain the stacks it has harvested, but will consume 10% (atleast 1) stack per day.
