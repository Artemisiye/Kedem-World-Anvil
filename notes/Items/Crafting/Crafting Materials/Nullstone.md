Nullstones (scientifically "Maginullium")
Soft clay-like material that can absorb [[Mana]] and nullify [[Spell|Spells]]. 
When it comes into contact with Mana, it undergoes a rapid crystallization process, becoming hard and brittle, eventually crumbling apart into dust.

Can be used as a [[Crafting]] component to grant anti-magic properties to [[Weapon]], [[armor]] and [[Building|buildings]].
