Kedem does not have a strict class system, but players will be able to develop their magical powers in different ways that fall into the classic mage archetypes.


#### Sorcerer
A sorcerer is a natural born magic user, capable of casting spells from the moment they step into Kedem. 
They develop their own [[Will Power]] and progress in the [[Arcana]] levels.

A player will be able to start as a sorcerer by picking an Arcana [[Starting Boon]]

### Warlocks and Clerics

Players will be able to become what is known as warlocks and clerics, essentially borrowing their powers from powerful being such as the [[God|Gods]], [[Titan|Titans]] and other entities such as [[Cthulhu]].

#### Warlock
Players will be able to strike contracts with entities in exchange for borrowed [[Will Power|magical powers]] and access to [[Ritual|Rituals]].

Warlock's power will be affected by their proximity to their Patron. The further away they are from the patron, the weaker it is. 
Possibly, the powers will be granted via wearable boon.
In order to maintain adequate powers, the warlocks can build [[Monuments]] for their patron, which will redirect some of the patron's power. Monuments can stack, resulting in a focal point of power.
#### Cleric
Players will be able to worship [[God|Gods]], and in times, when the gods have noticed them, they may bless them with magical powers or other boons.
