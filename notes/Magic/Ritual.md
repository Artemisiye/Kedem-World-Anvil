A ritual is a proccess that result in a magical effect or [[Spell]], but does not require [[Will Power|Magical or Will]], as it's source of magic is an external powerful being such as [[God|Gods]].



There will be a log of rituals cast, something like: 
_Kadmon "Rashaff" has invoked Osiris to cast Raise The Dead at 22.04.2025_
