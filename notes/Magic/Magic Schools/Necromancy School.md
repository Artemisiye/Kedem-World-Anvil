**Description**:: Necromancy spells involve death, undeath, and the manipulation of life energy. Prior to 3rd edition, healing spells are in the Necromancy school, however these spells are generally restricted to clerics and/or druids. Healing spells were shifted to the Conjuration school in 3rd edition, and Evocation in 5th edition.
