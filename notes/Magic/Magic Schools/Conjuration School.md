**Description**:: This school is focused on instantaneous transportation, conjuring manifestations of creatures, energy or objects, and object creation.  In earlier editions, this school was known as Conjuration/Summoning but became known as Conjuration in the 3rd and 3.5 editions. In 3rd and 3.5 editions, healing spells are also part of the conjuration school, however of the three core arcane classes, these spells are generally restricted to bards.
