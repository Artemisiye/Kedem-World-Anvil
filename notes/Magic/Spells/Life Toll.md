You point at one creature you can see within range, and the sound of a dolorous bell fills the air around it for a moment.
The caster sacrifices health to channel the spell, and upon release, attempts to even out the target's [[Health|HP]] to it's [[Vitality]], depending on the amount of health sacrificed.

School Class: [[Necromancy School|Necromancy]]
Source: [[Health]]
Casting Type: [[Channelling]]
