History / The First Age

The First Age of Light

Last updated: June 26, 2025 | Tags: Era, Creation

The First Age, often called the Age of Light, marks the dawn of recorded history, a time when the world was young and bathed in raw, untamed magic. It was an era of creation and immense possibility, preceding the great cataclysm known as [[The Sundering]]. The great [[Aethel the Star-Seeker]] theorized that during this period, the planes were more closely aligned, allowing for unprecedented flows of mana.

"To look upon the works of the First Age is to see the dreams of gods made manifest in stone and light. We are but children playing with the echoes of their power." — Inscription from the Sunken City of Oakhaven

The Rise of the Elder Races

It was during this epoch that the so-called Elder Races walked the land. Unlike the mortal races of the current age, they were beings of immense longevity and power, said to be shaped by the very essence of creation. The Luminai, masters of light and order, built crystalline cities that pierced the clouds. In the deep earth, the Geodes, beings of living stone, carved out intricate subterranean kingdoms. Their interactions, both peaceful and violent, shaped the very geography of the world.

The Seeds of Conflict

Yet, this idyllic age was not without its shadows. The same raw magic that fueled creation also gave rise to entities of chaos and void. The rise of [[Lord Malakor]] and his armies of shadow marked the beginning of the end for this era. His corrupting influence began to twist the fabric of magic, leading to the devastating conflict that would eventually trigger [[The Sundering]].
